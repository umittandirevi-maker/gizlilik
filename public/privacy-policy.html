Privacy Policy for FocusAI

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Developer Contact: fixbangstudio@gmail.com

FocusAI ("we", "our", or "us") provides the FocusAI mobile application for Android devices. We place paramount importance on your privacy, digital well-being, and personal data sovereignty.

Key Privacy Guarantee: FocusAI operates on a 100% on-device architecture. Your notifications, messages, alerts, and contacts are analyzed locally in volatile memory and are never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

1. Core Privacy Architecture: On-Device Processing

  • No Cloud Uploads: FocusAI does not transmit any incoming notification text, titles, application origins, or user data over the internet.
  • No External Logging: All filtering, keyword categorization, and prioritization logic are performed locally on your device.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Data: We do not sell, rent, license, or disclose your notification contents or personal information to third-party ad networks, telemetry services, or data brokers.

2. Sensitive Android Permissions & Usage

To perform notification filtering and critical emergency alerting, FocusAI requires specific Android permissions. Each permission is restricted strictly to its stated function:

A. Notification Listener Service (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

Why it is needed: Enables FocusAI to read incoming notifications in real-time.
How it is used: Analyzes incoming alerts against your custom priority rules and selected emergency keywords to determine if an urgent alert should sound or if non-urgent notifications should be silenced.
Data Retention: Processing occurs strictly in volatile device memory (RAM). Notification content is evaluated in milliseconds and immediately discarded; it is never stored persistently on disk or uploaded remotely.

B. Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Why it is needed: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and newer to post alerts.
How it is used: Delivers critical emergency notifications and alerts to you when prioritized events occur.

C. Notification Policy Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY) & Vibration (VIBRATE)

Why it is needed: Permits FocusAI to interact with device alert channels.
How it is used: Bypasses Do Not Disturb (DND) modes and triggers tactile vibrations exclusively for user-confirmed high-priority emergency conditions.

D. Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Why it is needed: Allows FocusAI to display installed applications.
How it is used: Used exclusively to populate your "Always Allow Apps" configuration screen with app names and icons so you can choose which apps bypass filtering. Package lists are never shared or transmitted.

3. Information Storage & Retention

Your local configuration settings—such as chosen emergency keywords, volume preferences, and selected "Always Allow" apps—are saved exclusively in the private sandboxed storage of your device (/data/data/com.aistudio.smartnotificationfilter.xrwv/). You can erase all data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app data in your device's Application Settings.

4. Third-Party Libraries & Tracking

FocusAI contains:

  • No Advertising SDKs: The application does not display advertisements.
  • No Tracking / Analytics SDKs: We do not track user behavior or create user profiles.
  • No Cloud Backend Dependencies: The core features run without requiring any remote server communication.

5. Controlling and Revoking Permissions

You maintain full control over FocusAI permissions and can revoke them at any time:

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Special App Access → Notification Access.
  3. Find FocusAI and toggle permission access off.

6. Children's Privacy

FocusAI does not target or knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The application operates solely as an on-device utility.
